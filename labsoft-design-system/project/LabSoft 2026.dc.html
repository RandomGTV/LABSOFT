LabSoft
Offline pathology software for one Windows PC at the reception counter. The report PDF is the product; every screen exists to reach it quickly and without a mistake.
Sixteen artboards at 1440 × 900, day and night. Set in Archivo on the Modernist grid: 0px radius, 2px section rules, red used only where an action or a danger lives.
